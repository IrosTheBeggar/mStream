mStream · Discover panel · design review · revision 2.2 · 16 Sep 2026

Discover Recommendation Modal

Clicking a row under From the network opens a window of plug-in sections. A row under From your peers opens on a Federation view built around the paired server’s song, album and artist, with a switch to the plug-in sections. Each card is a live page; open it at full size to read the small text.

builtthe window itself (branch feat/discovery-modal): previews, the invite row, play and queue from a peer, album and artist views, more-like-this on one peer, find-elsewhere links, copy title
planneddownloads, hand-offs, collection copies and their destination picker, the downloads tray
suggestedmy additions to the Federation view, easy to strike

Decided

  1. “Collection” means your library: the files are copied from the peer into a local folder the user sets or chooses, with {{ARTIST}}/{{ALBUM}} as the default. Card 10 shows the picker; card 02 shows the destination bar it feeds.

Four calls to make

  1. Copies go through the same stream proxy as playback, so the peer cannot tell them apart and the pairing key’s daily allowance is the only cap. Add a per-key “allow copies” switch (the client would declare intent with a header), or leave it to the allowance?
  2. Library rows: open the window too, or keep the one-click queue they have now?
  3. Where finished downloads live: a strip in the Discover panel (card 07), or a tab in the Upload window?
  4. Preview plug-ins ship on by default today. Keep that, or make them opt-in?

Needs API work first

  1. The peer's health route has to report the caller's transfer limits and usage before any allowance can be shown; today the peer only answers 429. The cards show "online · last seen" instead.
  2. The accepts-requests flag arrives with sidecar 1.1.0; until then the Federate hint cannot know the peer's inbox state.
  3. A "may create jobs" flag on the plug-in list, so Get it can be left out for a gated account instead of failing with a 403.
  4. User-settings routes and a Connected services page; only the database module exists, on the jobs branch, which itself is not on master yet.
  5. Collection copies: a federation-copy acquire plug-in on the jobs branch, a PEER variable in the torrent path-template engine, and the destination saved per account through the user-settings routes. The picker’s Browse… reuses the file explorer listing that exists today.
